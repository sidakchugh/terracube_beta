FEATURES

A survival sandbox that runs in a single browser tab and installs like an app.

❤️Survival

Hearts, hunger, food, drowning and fall damage. Eat to heal, sleep in a bed to skip the night.

🔨Real crafting

A 2×2 grid in your inventory and a 3×3 grid at the crafting table, with shaped recipes.

⛏️Tools and tiers

Wood, stone, iron and diamond pickaxes, axes, shovels and swords. Diamonds need an iron pickaxe.

🔥Furnace

Smelt iron, sand, cobblestone and raw meat using coal or wood as fuel.

🐖Animals and mobs

Pigs, cows and sheep by day. Zombies and creepers by night, and creepers really do blow holes in the ground.

🌍Big varied worlds

Oceans, beaches, deserts, forests, snowy peaks, caves, ores and deep diamonds. Every new world is different.

💾Worlds that save

Autosave in your browser, with a New World button and settings for mouse, field of view, distance and volume.

📱Install and play anywhere

Works as an installed app with touch controls on phones, and keyboard and mouse on desktop.

BLOCKS AND ITEMS

Forty-five blocks, items and tools to find, craft and build with.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Terracube is free and runs entirely in your browser.

Will my world be saved?

Yes. It autosaves in this browser every 20 seconds and when you pause or leave. Clearing the site's data deletes it.

Can I install it?

Yes. Use your browser's Install option, or Add to Home Screen on a phone. Once installed it opens like an app.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. On touch screens you get an on-screen stick, a look area and buttons for mine, use, jump and inventory.

Is there multiplayer?

Not yet. Terracube is a single-player game for now.

The game is slow. What can I do?

Lower the render distance in the pause menu, and use a recent Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox with hardware acceleration on.
